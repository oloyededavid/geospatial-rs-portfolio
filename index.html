About

Rigour, context,
and relevance.

Oloyede David Oluwayinka is a First Class B.Tech graduate in Remote Sensing & Geoscience Information Systems from the Federal University of Technology Akure (4.51/5.00), and the School of Earth and Mineral Sciences’ 4th best graduating student. His work focuses on urban heat dynamics, flood risk mapping, land-cover change, and climate-adaptive spatial modelling across West and Southern Africa.
