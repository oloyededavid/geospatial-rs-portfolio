Undergraduate Thesis+

Spatio-Temporal Assessment of Urban Heat Island Dynamics in Lagos State, Nigeria (2015–2024) Using Remote Sensing and GIS

Final Year Research Project · Dept. of Remote Sensing & GIS, FUTA · Supervisor: Prof. Akinola Komolafe

2025

Lagos is one of Africa's fastest-growing megacities — and one of its hottest. This study assessed a decade of rapid urban expansion and how it reshaped the thermal environment of Nigeria's economic center.

  • Processed Landsat 7, 8, and 9 imagery to assess 2015–2024 urban heat dynamics, retrieving Land Surface Temperature using the mono-window algorithm.
  • Derived NDVI, NDWI, and NDBI and performed supervised LULC classification using Random Forest in Google Earth Engine and QGIS.
  • Quantified relationships between vegetation loss, urban expansion, impervious surfaces, and surface temperature, finding a 2.4°C increase in mean surface temperature and substantial conversion of previously vegetated areas to built-up surfaces.
